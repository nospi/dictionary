How to Play

  1. Create or join a room - Get your friends together (3-10 players)
  2. Get an obscure word - The game shows everyone a weird word
  3. Write a fake definition - Try to make it sound believable!
  4. Vote for the real definition - Guess which one is correct
  5. Score points - Earn points for guessing right or fooling others
